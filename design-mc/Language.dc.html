The board’s vocabulary
Coverage is a fill. Events are pins.
Keeping the two apart is what stops the map turning into a colour salad. A block’s fill answers one question only — has anyone walked this? Anything found sits on top as a pin, so it survives the block being searched later.
Block fill — who has walked it
Nobody yetUNSEARCHED
The default. Barely visible, so the map reads as work-done rather than work-left.
Someone’s on itIN_PROGRESS
Claimed. Dashed because it isn’t settled yet. Carries their initial, and nobody else is sent here.
WalkedSEARCHED
Someone covered it and said so. The green spreading across the map is the whole reward loop.
Worth another lookNEEDS_REVISIT
Walked, but in the dark, or in rain, or fast. Ages back toward unsearched on its own.
Ruled outCLOSED
Fenced, private, or across the motorway. Dimmed so it stops asking to be searched.
Pins — what happened where
Sighting
Halo size and opacity track how fresh it is. An hour-old sighting shouts; a week-old one whispers.
ClueCLUE_FOUND
Fur, a collar, a print. A pin rather than a fill, so the block can still be marked searched.
Flyer up
Dropped where it was actually taped. Shows the coverage gap posters have, not just blocks.
Found himPET_FOUND
The only solid fill on the map. Ends the search and everyone watching sees it land.
A person, right now
One colour each, matching the block they hold. Seeing someone else move is what makes it feel joint.
Every state above is already in the database. GridCell carries the status, the priority, who claimed it and when it was searched; FlyerPosting, PetSpotting and SearchArea carry the pins. None of it is on the map today — the five tables are empty and no Mission Control component reads them.
