# Monetization Principles (decided 2026-06-10)

The goal is permanence, not profit: enough revenue that the platform is
forever operational for the benefit of the world.

## Free forever
- Medication tracking, for every pet, including shared care teams.
  (Competitors paywall exactly this: PetTimely charges $7.99/mo for family
  sharing. Free here is the differentiator and the daily-habit funnel that
  keeps recovery profiles accurate.)
- Lost pet recovery itself. Reporting, alerts, matching, community search.
  Recovery is never paywalled: money only ever buys extra megaphone.

## Paid
- Boosted reach for a lost pet alert: we run Facebook/Instagram ads built
  automatically from the pet's profile (photos, canonical colors, last-seen
  geo-targeting). Pricing: flat $5 service fee + ad spend passed through at
  cost. Auto-pause the campaign the moment the pet is marked found.

## Future candidates (not yet committed)
- Shelter management software, around $25/month per shelter, riding the
  same platform at marginal cost.
- Tasteful, clearly-labeled sponsorships from pet companies. Subtle and
  with class; never interfering with recovery or medication flows.

## Naming note
"Rescue Squad" is PawBoost's trademark for their volunteer network. All
user-facing surfaces and public URLs now say "Rescue Force(s)"
(old /rescue-squads URLs 301-redirect). Internal identifiers (DB models,
/api/rescue-squads paths, component names) are not publicly visible and
are deferred; see HANDOFF.md.
