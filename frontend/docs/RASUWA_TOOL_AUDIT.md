- **The emergency-disclosure ask (founder direction, 2026-09-02: get
  the missing people's last known locations through provider
  emergency disclosure):** every letter, person and general, on every
  path, now carries a fourth ask. Under 18 U.S.C. 2702, Google,
  Apple, Meta, and the phone carriers may hand a person's last known
  device location to a governmental entity in a danger-of-death
  emergency, and missing persons qualify under the providers' own
  policies; the request must come from law enforcement, which is why
  the letters ask for a missing person case first. US letters ask
  the FBI (the general letter, the FBI and the Department of
  Justice); Canadian letters the RCMP or local police; international
  letters the national police; Nepal letters Nepal Police, with
  every result routed to the officers coordinating the search. The
  US call guidance adds the family-side move: any local police
  department can open the case and send the emergency disclosure
  request today. Template v7 rebuilds older hand-edited drafts.
